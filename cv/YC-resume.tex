\job{SCALAR Lab, UMass Amherst \textbar\ Graduate Researcher (advisor: Scott Niekum)}{Amherst, MA}{2022 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{HC-RLHF (RLC 2025):} built the first RLHF method with a \textbf{provable safety guarantee}, returning an unsafe model with
        probability \hl{at most $\delta$}; trained Qwen and Llama models (1.5B--3B) with a variance-aware RLOO policy gradient,
        improving harmlessness and helpfulness over Safe RLHF, with \hl{0 of 30} trials returning an unsafe model.
  \item \textbf{LARA (COLM 2026):} made any decoder safety-constrained at \textbf{inference time with no weight updates} by calibrating one
        Lagrange multiplier in \hl{about two minutes}; Best-of-$N$ with LARA approaches finetuned baselines.
  \item \textbf{Scaling laws (NeurIPS 2024):} showed that DPO, IPO, and SLiC overoptimize like classical RLHF across 1B--6.9B models,
        overfitting within a single epoch and drifting toward out-of-distribution responses.
  \item Developed adaptive-margin DPO from preference-over-preference data, and risk-sensitive alignment via stochastic dominance.
\end{itemize}
